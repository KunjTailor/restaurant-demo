Pickup

Order ahead, skip the wait

Pickup runs from 11:30 am until 15 minutes before close. Most orders are ready in about 20 minutes; on Friday and Saturday nights, give us 30.

Start a pickup order Or call it in

  1. Order online or by phone

    Pay ahead online, or call and pay when you arrive. Add special requests, like well-done crust or sauce on the side, at checkout.

  2. We text you when it’s ready

    Your order goes in the oven right before your pickup time, so it’s hot when you arrive, not sitting under a heat lamp.

  3. Grab it inside the blue door

    Pickup shelves are just to the left of the host stand. Staying in the car? Call when you pull up and we’ll run it out.

Delivery

Delivered within four miles

We partner with DoorDash and Uber Eats from 11:30 am to 9 pm daily. Pizzas ride in vented boxes so the crust stays crisp, and pasta travels in sealed tins you can reheat right in the oven.

Order on DoorDash Order on Uber Eats

Delivery prices and fees are set by each app. Ordering for 15 or more? Catering is cheaper and we deliver it ourselves.

Good to know

Questions we get a lot

Something else? Call us at (503) 555-0143 during open hours.

Do you take walk-ins?

Always. The bar, the patio and about a third of the dining room are held for walk-ins. On weekend nights the wait is usually 20 to 40 minutes; put your name down and we’ll text you when your table’s ready.

Can I get a gluten-free pizza?

Yes. Any pizza can be made on our gluten-free crust for $3, and any pasta can be swapped for gluten-free penne for $2. They’re cooked in a shared kitchen and oven, so we don’t recommend them for celiac guests.

Is there anything vegan?

The Marinara and our wood-roasted olives are vegan as they are. Most pizzas can be made vegan with cashew mozzarella for $2, and the kitchen can adapt several pastas. Just ask.

Can I bring my own wine?

Sure. Corkage is $20 per bottle, and we waive it for each bottle you buy from our list.

Are dogs allowed?

Well-behaved dogs are welcome on the patio, and we’ll bring out a water bowl.

Do you have half-and-half pizzas?

For pickup and delivery, yes: pick any two pies from the same section of the menu and we’ll charge for the higher one. In the dining room we keep them whole so they bake evenly.
