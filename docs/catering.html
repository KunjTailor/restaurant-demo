Catering FAQ

The fine print, in plain words

How much notice do you need?

48 hours for most orders. For full-service events or anything over 75 guests, two weeks is safer. Last-minute? Call us; if the oven has room, we’ll make it happen.

Where do you deliver?

Anywhere within 10 miles of the restaurant for a flat $25, which covers most of Portland, plus Milwaukie and Beaverton. Farther out, we’ll quote it.

Is there a minimum order?

$250 before tax for delivery. Pickup orders have no minimum.

Can you handle allergies?

Every tray is labeled, and we pack gluten-free and vegan dishes separately. Our kitchen does handle wheat, dairy, nuts and shellfish, so tell us about serious allergies and we’ll talk you through the options.

What if we need to cancel?

Cancel more than 7 days out and we refund your deposit in full. Inside 7 days we keep the deposit, but it becomes credit for any future order.
