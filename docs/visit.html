Find the blue door

We’re on SE Alder between 21st and 22nd, in the old bike shop with the arched blue door. Follow the smell of wood smoke.

Portland, OR 97214

Getting here

  • ParkingFree two-hour street parking on Alder and the side streets. After 5 pm our lot behind the building is free for guests; the entrance is on SE 21st.

  • TransitBus 15 stops at SE Belmont & 20th, a four-minute walk. Bus 20 on E Burnside is about ten minutes away.

  • BikesSix staple racks right out front, and a covered spot by the patio for cargo bikes.

  • AccessibilityStep-free entrance through the patio gate, accessible restroom, and tables at wheelchair height throughout the dining room.

Before you come

A few things regulars know

  • Beat the wait

    Friday and Saturday from 6 to 8 pm is our busiest stretch. Come before 5:30 or after 8:30 and you’ll usually walk right in.

  • Sit at the counter

    The six stools facing the oven are walk-in only, and the best seats in the house for watching pies go in.

  • Bring the kids

    High chairs, crayons, a $9 kids’ menu, and a ball of dough to play with if you ask nicely.

  • Dogs welcome outside

    Well-behaved dogs are welcome on the patio, where there’s always a water bowl by the gate.
