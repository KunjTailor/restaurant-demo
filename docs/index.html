How we make it

Every pie starts two days before you order it

Our dough is just flour, water, sea salt and a pinch of yeast, left to ferment for 48 hours so it bakes up light, blistered and easy to digest. Pasta gets the same patience: rolled and cut by hand before lunch service, every single day.
